Settings

Recent-winner penalty

A child chosen recently is docked for the next few weeks: −N last week, −(N−1) the week before, easing by one each week until it disappears after N weeks. (Default N = 5)

Weeks applied (N)
5

Attendance bonus

Rewards a child who keeps being nominated. Every week on the roster within the recent weeks window earns the bonus, and a missed week only costs that week — it never resets the count.
